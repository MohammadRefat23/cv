\section{Research Interests}

\textbf{Keywords:}
Computational Astrophysics, Scientific Computing, Time-Series Analysis, Inverse Problems, Bayesian Inference, Statistical Modeling, Brown Dwarf Atmospheres, Stellar Surface Mapping

\vspace{0.3em}

\textbf{Executive Summary:}
I am a computational astrophysicist working at the intersection of scientific computing and statistical inference. I am interested in applying time-series analysis, inverse methods, and Bayesian inference to interpret both astrophysical simulations and observational data. I apply these computational approaches to problems including stellar surface reconstruction, brown dwarf and giant exoplanet atmospheres, and Galactic stellar populations, with the goal of developing robust methods for extracting physical insight from complex astronomical datasets.
